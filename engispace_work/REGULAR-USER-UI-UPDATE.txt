ENGISPACE - OCCUPANCY-BASED USER WORKFLOW

- Regular-user navigation focuses on Dashboard and Room Availability.
- Users can see vacant and occupied classrooms.
- Occupy Room opens a form for Program, Section, Instructor, Subject, and Purpose.
- Program, Section, and Purpose are required for every room.
- Instructor and Subject are optional in regular classrooms and required in laboratories.
- Time In is recorded automatically when occupancy begins.
- Leave Room requires a classroom photo; Time Out is recorded automatically after successful submission.
- Users can review their own occupancy history.
- Classroom management remains Admin-only.
- Reservation pages, actions, and backend reservation logic are not part of this workflow.
