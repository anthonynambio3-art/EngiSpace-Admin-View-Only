ENGISPACE - ROOM AVAILABILITY AND ROOM TYPES

1. Room Availability lists classrooms grouped by the saved classroom records.
2. Vacant rooms show an Occupy Room action.
3. Occupied rooms show the occupant and Time In.
4. Only the owner of an active occupancy sees the Leave Room action.
5. Admins can classify each classroom as Regular Classroom or Laboratory in Classroom Management.
6. Program, Section, and Purpose are required for all occupancies.
7. Instructor and Subject are optional for regular classrooms, but required for laboratories.
8. The system does not provide reservations, advance booking, or future time-slot selection.
