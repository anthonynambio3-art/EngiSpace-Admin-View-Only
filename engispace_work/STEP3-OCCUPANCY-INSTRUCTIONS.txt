ENGISPACE - ACTUAL CLASSROOM OCCUPANCY

There is no reservation or advance-booking feature in this version.

REGULAR USER WORKFLOW
1. Open Room Availability.
2. Choose a vacant classroom and click Occupy Room.
3. Enter Program, Section, and Purpose (required).
4. Instructor and Subject are optional for regular classrooms.
5. For laboratory rooms, Instructor and Subject are both required.
6. Confirm Occupancy. The server records the logged-in user and Time In automatically.
7. When leaving, click Leave Room and upload a photo of the cleaned classroom.
8. After a valid photo is submitted, the server records Time Out and marks the room vacant.

RULES
- Only one active occupancy is allowed per classroom.
- Only the user who started an occupancy can end it.
- The exit photo is mandatory; JPG, PNG, and WEBP are accepted up to 5 MB.
- Users cannot manually set Time In or Time Out.
- Completed records remain in the user's occupancy history.
- Admin can view all occupancy records and inspect submitted photos.
